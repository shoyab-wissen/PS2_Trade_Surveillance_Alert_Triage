# Compliance Standards and Regulatory Requirements

## Applicable Regulations

### SEC Regulations

**Rule 10b-5 (Anti-Fraud)**
- Prohibition on market manipulation and deceptive practices
- Applies to all securities and markets
- Requires reasonable surveillance procedures

**Rule 15c2-1 (Book and Records)**
- Requirement to maintain books and records of all transactions
- 6-year minimum retention for most records
- Accessible within 1 business day for SEC examination

### FINRA Rules

**Rule 5210 (Market Surveillance)**
- Requirement for market surveillance program
- Must detect patterns of potential market manipulation
- Must document detection methodology and findings
- Regular reporting to FINRA

**Rule 4530 (Customers - Options Accounts)**
- Suitability and account monitoring requirements
- Applies to options trading activities

### NASDAQ Rules

**5600 Series (Market Rules)**
- Manipulation and disruptive trading practices
- Specifically addresses layering, spoofing, painting the tape

## Internal Audit Standards

### SOX Compliance (Section 404)

**Internal Controls Over Financial Reporting (ICFR)**
- Market surveillance is part of financial reporting controls
- Must document control procedures
- Annual audit and attestation required
- Issues must be remediated timely

### Documentation Standards

**What Must Be Documented**:
- Detection methodology and algorithms
- Each detection with supporting evidence
- Confidence calculations and thresholds
- Investigation decisions and findings
- Final disposition and actions taken

**How Long to Keep**:
- Active investigations: Throughout investigation + 6 months after
- Regulatory hold: 7 years minimum
- Routine cases: 3 years after closure

## Report Content Requirements

### Compliance Report Minimum Content

✅ Executive summary with key findings
✅ Detailed description of each detection
✅ Statistical evidence supporting conclusions
✅ Methodology explanation
✅ Confidence/certainty levels
✅ Investigation timeline and status
✅ Actions taken and decisions made
✅ Audit trail with timestamps
✅ Preparer identification and signature
✅ Compliance officer review/approval
✅ Distribution and retention notes

### Evidence Standards

**Evidence Must Include**:
- Specific trade details (date, time, security, size, price)
- Trader identification
- Order flow sequence with timestamps
- Statistical calculations and significance tests
- Comparison to peer group or baselines
- Cross-reference to market conditions/news

**Evidence Chain**:
- Traceability from detection to source trades
- No gaps in logic or reasoning
- Alternative explanations considered
- Counterarguments documented if applicable

## Record Retention Matrix

| Record Type | Duration | Location | Accessible |
|-------------|----------|----------|-----------|
| Detection logs | 3 years | Active archive | Same-day |
| Investigation files | 7 years | Hold storage | Within 1 business day |
| Compliance reports | 7 years | Vault storage | Within 2 business days |
| Regulatory filings | Permanent | Archives | Within 5 business days |
| System audit logs | 2 years | Active systems | Real-time |

## Regulatory Examination Preparation

**SEC/FINRA May Request**:
- Surveillance procedures documentation
- Detection methodology description
- Sample of detected cases with evidence
- Investigation files for selected cases
- Training records for analysts
- System validation/testing documentation
- Thresholds and parameter rationale

**Best Practices**:
- Keep procedures up-to-date and accessible
- Maintain training records (annual refresher)
- Test system accuracy (quarterly)
- Document threshold changes and rationale
- Track enforcement actions and resolutions
- Maintain communication logs with regulators

## Confidentiality and Distribution Controls

**High Sensitivity**:
- Investigation files (pre-conclusion)
- Regulatory communications
- Disciplinary decisions

**Internal Only**:
- Compliance reports
- Market intelligence
- Performance metrics

**External Distribution**:
- Regulatory filings (to SEC/FINRA)
- Investigation closure notices (to traders)
- Enforcement actions (if applicable)

## Regulatory Filing Format

For SEC submission use:
- Form 15C2-1 (Books and Records)
- Or narrative letter format with exhibits
- Include methodology description
- Include sample detections
- Include investigation outcomes

For FINRA submission use:
- 4530 Surveillance Report
- Annual certification of procedures
- Any unusual patterns or findings
